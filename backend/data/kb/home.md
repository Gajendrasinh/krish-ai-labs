# Krish AI Labs — Overview

Krish AI Labs is an AI & software engineering studio. Tagline: "Building Intelligent Software
for the Future." We design and build full-stack applications, AI agents, and RAG-powered products
— from first prototype to cloud-scale deployment. We work with both enterprise and startup clients.

## What We Do

- **AI & GenAI Development** — LLM-powered features, copilots, and generative tools built into
  your product.
- **Full-Stack Development** — End-to-end web and SaaS applications, from database to interface.
- **AI Agents & Automation** — Autonomous agents that handle workflows, support, and repetitive
  tasks.
- **RAG & LLM Applications** — Retrieval-augmented systems that ground AI answers in your own
  data.
- **Cloud Solutions** — Scalable, secure infrastructure and deployment on AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- **Technical Consulting** — Architecture reviews, tech strategy, and hands-on IT advisory.

## How We Work

1. **Discover** — Understand your goals, users, and technical constraints.
2. **Design** — Architect the system and map out the build plan.
3. **Build** — Ship in iterative cycles, with regular working demos.
4. **Deploy & Support** — Launch to production and stay on for iteration and support.
